This paper presents a training framework for heterogeneous robots performing unique, sequential, and collaborative tasks, including policy transfer from single-task learning to multi-task collaboration.

* Signed Type Encoding with curriculum learning significantly outperformed one-hot and no-encoding baselines (p < 0.05, seven independent runs).
* The approach enabled collaborative behavior that training from scratch failed to learn.
* Presented at ICCRE 2026, Japan. Supported by the National Science Foundation (Award No. 2302060) through the ROSE project, and co-authored with participating high school teachers.
