Multi-agent AI decisions are hard for people to inspect. This paper introduces PEIM (Policy Explanation in MARL), a model-agnostic framework that explains both individual and collaborative agent decisions.

* Tested on the StarCraft Multi-Agent Challenge (SMACv2).
* Revealed team tactics such as grouping, focus fire, and fleeing, without retraining the policies.
