PEIM (Policy Explanation in MARL) explains why trained agents act, at every step and over time, without retraining the policy. It fits local surrogate models around each decision, ranks the most influential observation features, and shows them in a custom visual interface alongside the agents' recent actions.

On the StarCraft Multi-Agent Challenge (SMACv2) it revealed human-like tactics: grouping, focus fire, fleeing, target selection, divide and conquer, and surround-and-kill.

**Role:** first author; designed, built and evaluated the framework. **Paper:** [IEEE Xplore](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICTAI66417.2025.00194)
