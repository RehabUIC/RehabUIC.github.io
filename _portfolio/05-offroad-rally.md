A semi-realistic vehicle physics system for high-speed off-road terrain, plus an assistance system that blends player input with AI control based on steering, stability and road curvature. It corrects viable actions but lets mistakes play out, so success still feels earned. Opponents follow paths and recover physically when knocked off track.

**Role:** lead programmer: vehicle physics, all driving modes, AI navigation, cameras and effects.
