Each roll is built at runtime: a procedural mesh, a skeleton, and a network of configurable joints whose mass, tension and elasticity adapt to joint position and roll size. Vertex skin weights come from vertex-to-joint distance, like smooth skin binding in animation tools. Transfers between containers generate curved connections and animate the mesh in real time, with no pre-authored animation.

**Role:** sole programmer: physics, mesh generation, gameplay and interaction.
