I am a Ph.D. student in the [Electronic Visualization Laboratory (EVL)](https://www.evl.uic.edu/) at the University of Illinois Chicago, advised by Dr. Saeed Boorboor. My research is in AI and immersive visualization, with a focus on 3D displays and AR/VR.

**I am open to Summer 2027 research and engineering internships in XR, real-time graphics and applied AI.** [Download my CV](/files/Rehab_Uddin_Shawon_CV.pdf) or [email me](mailto:rshaw610@uic.edu).

Before UIC, I earned my M.S. in Computer Science at Missouri State University, where I worked with Dr. Siming Liu on **scalable and explainable multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) for robot teams**. That work tackled two obstacles to deploying robot teams in warehouse automation, disaster response, and service robotics: coordination methods that break down as the number of robot and task types grows, and AI decisions that people cannot inspect.

Research highlights
======
* **Scalable heterogeneous coordination.** I developed a shared-policy MARL framework with a priority-aware grid sensor that scales to ten robot and task types, a setting where the standard Unity ML-Agents baseline fails to learn. The policies also generalize to unseen task sequences and collaboration requirements without retraining. *(IEEE ICTAI 2026, accepted)*
* **Explainable multi-agent AI.** I built PEIM (Policy Explanation in MARL), a model-agnostic framework that explains individual and team decisions. On the StarCraft Multi-Agent Challenge, it revealed tactics such as grouping, focus fire, and fleeing. *(IEEE ICTAI 2025)*
* **Research with educators.** Through the NSF-funded ROSE project (Award No. 2302060), I co-taught machine learning to high school teachers and co-authored a journal paper with three of them. *(IJMERR 2026)*
